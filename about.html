OUR STORY

A warm welcome to Tsagaan Garid.

A place to pause in Terkh, Tariat, Arkhangai — and a starting point for your own Mongolian journey.

A STAY WITH A SENSE OF PLACE

Simple comforts. Meaningful experiences.

Tsagaan Garid brings together countryside accommodation and personally arranged journeys. With 10 Mongolian gers and 10 A-frame cabins, we welcome independent travellers, couples, families and groups.

Enjoy good food and everyday comforts, then discover more through visits to herding families and beautiful natural places. We’ll talk through the details with you so your visit fits your interests.
