GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful answers.

How do I book?

Send a trip inquiry or call +976 9494 7544. We’ll discuss availability, arrangements and price with you. Your booking is confirmed only after we agree the details directly.

Can I visit alone, with children or in a group?

We welcome independent travellers, couples, families and groups. Tell us your party size, children’s ages and any needs so we can discuss suitable arrangements.

What facilities are available?

The camp has 10 gers, 10 A-frame cabins, food, hot water and bathroom facilities. Please confirm bed arrangements, bathroom access and meal details with us before booking.

Are meals and experiences included in the price?

The price and inclusions are discussed for your stay. We’ll agree accommodation, meals, experiences and any transport arrangements with you before confirmation.

How do I find the camp?

We are in Terkh, 1st bag, Tariat soum, Arkhangai. Contact us for the exact camp pin and directions before travelling. The map link below shows the general Tariat area.

Can you help plan an itinerary?

Yes. We can plan a personal itinerary, including visits to local herding families and beautiful natural places. Share your interests and available time.

What should I confirm before arrival?

Please agree dates, price, payment and cancellation terms, directions and any dietary or accessibility needs directly with us. Ask about seasonal opening and current travel conditions for your dates.
